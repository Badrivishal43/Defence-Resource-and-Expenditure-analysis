Global Military Expenditure Analytics

Project Overview

An Excel-based data analytics project analyzing global military expenditure across countries and years.

The project focuses on identifying spending trends, regional differences, top-spending countries, and year-over-year changes in military expenditure.

Dashboard

The interactive dashboard includes:

- Total Military Expenditure
- Years Covered
- Top Spending Country
- Highest Spending Region
- Top 10 Countries by Military Expenditure
- Military Expenditure by Region
- Global Military Expenditure Trend
- Year-on-Year Military Expenditure Growth
- Year slicer for interactive filtering

Excel Skills Used

- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Slicers
- Excel formulas
- Data cleaning
- Data modeling
- Dashboard design
- Year-over-Year analysis
- KPI reporting

Data Model

The workbook follows a simple dimensional structure:

- `Dim_Country` – Country, CountryKey, and Region
- `Dim_Year` – Year dimension
- `Fact_Military_Expenditure` – Military expenditure by country and year

Key Insights

- The United States is the highest-spending country in the dataset.
- North America is the highest-spending region.
- Global military expenditure shows a strong long-term upward trend.
- Year-over-year growth varies significantly across different periods.
